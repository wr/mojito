How to send GIFs on a Mac

Messages has GIF search built in; most other Mac apps don’t. Here’s how to send a GIF that actually moves, from any app.

By Wells Riley, who makes Mojito·Updated October 5, 2026·Tested on macOS 27

+apps #images → GIF

In Messages: the Apps button left of the message field, then #images. Search, click, press Return.

How do I send a GIF in Messages on a Mac?

  1. Open a conversation and click the Apps button (+) left of the message field.
  2. Choose #images.
  3. Search or pick a trending GIF, click it, and press Return to send.

#images works in 13 countries, including the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and India. Missing? Check System SettingsGeneralLanguage & Region.

Its GIFs animate only for Apple users. To keep one you received, drag it to your desktop; Save sends it to Photos.

Mojito brings GIF search to other apps: type :::party in Mail or a browser and press Return.

How do I send GIFs in Slack, Discord, Mail, and other apps?

  • Slack: click the smiley in the message field, choose GIFs, and search. It’s powered by Giphy, and admins can turn it off.
  • Discord: click GIF in the message bar. Results now come from KLIPY.
  • Mail: drag a .gif file into the message. Classic Outlook for Windows shows only the first frame.
  • Websites: use the site’s GIF button, or drag the file into the compose box.
  • Messages, Notes, Pages, Keynote: drag a .gif file in like any image.
  • Any app, with Mojito: type :::party and press Return to paste a real .gif at your cursor. Works in Slack and Discord too.

How do I get a real .gif from Giphy or Tenor?

Save Image As often gives you a .webp. Browsers play it; many apps won’t accept or animate it.

  1. On giphy.com, right-click the GIF and choose Open Image in New Tab.
  2. Change .webp at the end of the address to .gif, press Return, and save it.
  3. Drag the file from Downloads into your message. Dragging sends the file itself, which keeps it moving.

On Tenor, save the big image on the GIF’s own page; it’s a real .gif. Since Google shut down the Tenor API on June 30, 2026, that’s how Tenor GIFs reach Mac apps: what happened to Tenor.

Why do GIFs arrive frozen?

  • It was pasted as a picture. Many apps, Slack and Discord included, keep only the first frame of a copied image. Drag or paste the file instead.
  • It was a link. A giphy.com page address may arrive as a card or a bare link.
  • It wasn’t a GIF. A .webp or .mp4 can play in a browser and nowhere else.
  • Motion is off. With Auto-play animated images off in System SettingsAccessibilityMotion, your Mac pauses GIFs in Messages and Safari.

More GIF questions

Is there a GIF keyboard for Mac?

Not built in, and unlike iPhone, the Mac doesn’t take add-on keyboards like Gboard. GIF search lives inside apps like Messages, Slack, and Discord; tools that add it everywhere else are compared in the best emoji apps for Mac.

Do I need a Giphy or Tenor account?

No. Searching and saving GIFs on giphy.com and tenor.com works without signing in.
