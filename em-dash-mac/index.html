How to type an em dash on a Mac (—)

Hold Shift and Option, press Hyphen. That one shortcut works in every app on your Mac. Below: four other ways in, the en dash it’s easily confused with, and the setting that types dashes for you whether you asked or not.

By Wells Riley, who makes Mojito·Updated October 4, 2026·Tested on macOS 27

⇧shift ⌥option -hyphen → —

What’s the em dash shortcut on a Mac?

Press Shift + Option + Hyphen (the key just right of 0). macOS types an em dash, —, wherever your cursor is: Pages, Gmail in Chrome, Slack, Terminal, a filename in Finder. Leave out Shift, so it’s Option + Hyphen, and you get the shorter en dash, –.

It works everywhere because it isn’t an app feature. The dash is printed on your keyboard layout itself, one layer down, the same way Shift + 8 is an asterisk. These shortcuts are for the standard US layout; other layouts can move keys around, so if nothing happens, check the keyboard map further down.

With Mojito, you can type ::em_dash (or just ::em and pick it from the list). Shortcodes don’t care which keyboard layout you use.

5 ways to type an em dash on a Mac

  1. The shortcut: ⇧ ⌥ -. Fastest once it’s in your fingers, and it works in every app.
  2. Two hyphens: in Apple’s own apps (TextEdit, Notes, Mail, Messages), typing -- becomes — on its own when Smart Dashes is on. Handy, but it does nothing in Chrome, Slack, VS Code, or most cross-platform apps.
  3. The Character Viewer: press Fn + E (or Control + Command + Space), click the expand button in the panel’s top-right corner, search for em dash, and double-click it.
  4. A text replacement: in System SettingsKeyboardText Replacements…, click +, and map something like ;em to —. It syncs to your iPhone through iCloud, but like Smart Dashes, it only fires in apps built on Apple’s text system.
  5. A shortcode: with Mojito installed, type ::em_dash in any text field. A picker appears as you type, so ::em plus Return is enough.

Em dash, en dash, or hyphen?

They look alike and do three different jobs. Here’s the cheat sheet:

—Em dash⇧ ⌥ -A break in a sentence—like this::em_dash
–En dash⌥ -Ranges: Mon–Fri, pages 9–14::en_dash
-Hyphen-Joined words: well-known
−Minus signNoneMath: 5 − 3::minus_sign

The em dash is the loud one. It sets off an aside, marks a sudden turn, or stands in for a colon when you want more punch. The en dash, half its width, means “to” or “versus”: 1990–2000, the New York–London flight, a 3–1 win. The hyphen joins words that work as one. Using a hyphen as a range (Mon-Fri) is the most common slip in otherwise careful writing, and an easy one to fix now that you know ⌥ -.

Why does -- turn into — by itself?

That’s macOS’s Smart Dashes substitution, the sibling of smart quotes. In apps that use Apple’s text system, two hyphens in a row become an em dash as you type. If you’re writing code, a command-line flag like --force, or Markdown, that’s exactly what you don’t want.

  • One app: in that app’s menu bar, choose EditSubstitutionsSmart Dashes to toggle it.
  • Everywhere: open System SettingsKeyboardText InputEdit… and turn off Use smart quotes and dashes. It’s one switch for both, so straight quotes come back too.
  • Pages, Numbers, Keynote: they keep their own list under PagesSettingsAuto-Correction.
  • Microsoft Word: Word ignores the macOS switch. Look under WordPreferencesAutoCorrectAutoFormat As You Type for the option that replaces hyphens with dashes.

The reverse problem, a stubborn hyphen that won’t become a dash, usually means the app isn’t using Apple’s text system at all. The keyboard shortcut still works there; the substitution never will.

How do I type an em dash on iPhone or iPad?

Switch to the 123 keyboard, then press and hold the hyphen key. A small menu slides up with the en dash (–), the em dash (—), and a bullet (•); slide to the one you want and let go.

More dash questions

Should there be spaces around an em dash?

Depends on whose rules you follow. AP style, common in news writing, puts a space on each side ( — ). The Chicago Manual of Style and most book publishers close it up (—). Neither is wrong; pick one per document and stick to it.

Does using em dashes make my writing look AI-generated?

Chatbots lean on the em dash heavily, so some readers now treat it as a tell. It’s a bad heuristic: the mark has been doing honest work in English since long before computers. If a sentence reads better with one, use it. If you’re reaching for one in every paragraph, that’s worth a second look regardless of who’s reading.

What’s the HTML code for an em dash?

&mdash; or &#8212; for — (Unicode U+2014), and &ndash; or &#8211; for – (U+2013).

Why do three hyphens in Markdown make a line instead?

On a line by itself, --- is Markdown for a horizontal rule. Directly under a line of text, it turns that text into a heading. Neither has anything to do with dashes; type ⇧⌥- for a real em dash in Markdown.

How do I type an em dash on Windows?

Hold Alt and type 0151 on the numeric keypad (0150 for the en dash), or press Win + . and look under Symbols.
