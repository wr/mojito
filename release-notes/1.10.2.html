New

  • Site exceptions now apply in five more browsers people are moving to from Arc: Search, Ora, Nook, Ardia, and Crest. (#253)
  • Symbols you use now show up in Quick Access, the way emoji do. (#247)

Fixed

  • Clearing a half-typed shortcode with Cmd+A then Delete, Cmd+Delete, or Option+Delete no longer stops the next : from opening the picker. (#255)
  • Cmd+A, Cmd+X, Cmd+V, and Cmd+Z are now recognized on French, German, Dvorak, and other non-US keyboard layouts. (#256)
  • The Quick Access grid in Settings updates right away when you turn Symbols on or off, and turning Symbols on no longer delays the first :. (#248)
