How to type emoji on a Mac

Every Mac has an emoji picker one shortcut away. Here are seven ways in, from the Edit menu to typing :tada: like in Slack.

By Wells Riley of Wells Workshop, maker of Mojito·Updated October 5, 2026·Tested on macOS 27

🌐fn E → 😀

or ⌃ Control ⌘ Command Space on any keyboard

What’s the emoji shortcut on a Mac?

Press Fn + E (the 🌐 key, bottom left). The picker opens at your cursor: type to search, then click an emoji or press Return.

No Fn key? Control + Command + Space opens the same picker. Neither needs any setup.

Mojito lets you skip the picker: type :tada and press Return for 🎉, like in Slack.

  • Fn + E or ⌃⌘Space. Fn + E is the shortcut Apple documents today, and the picker keeps your recent emoji on top. ⌃ ⌘ Space is the original, for keyboards with no Fn key.
  • Tap 🌐 alone. In System SettingsKeyboard, set Press 🌐 key to Show Emoji & Symbols, then tap 🌐 by itself.
  • Type a word, then Fn + E. Write heart or coffee and press Fn + E. macOS (Sonoma or later) offers matching emoji that replace the word.
  • Edit › Emoji & Symbols. At the bottom of nearly every app’s Edit menu, for when the shortcut slips your mind.
  • Text replacements. In System SettingsKeyboardText Replacements…, map ;tada to 🎉. Syncs to your iPhone, but needs a rule per emoji and only works in Apple’s apps.
  • Shortcodes. With Mojito, type :fire and press Return, or :fire: to skip the picker. It searches by meaning: :yay brings up 🥳, 🙌 and 🎉.

Skin tones, favorites, and other picker tricks

  • Skin tone: click and hold an emoji, then choose a tone. macOS remembers it per emoji.
  • Favorites: click the expand button (top right), select an emoji, and click Add to Favorites.
  • Genmoji: on Macs with Apple Intelligence, the Genmoji button makes a custom emoji from a description.

macOS Tahoe 26.4 (March 2026) added Emoji 17.0, including Orca 🫍, Trombone 🪊 and Treasure Chest 🪎. On older systems they show up as empty boxes.

Common questions

Why won’t the emoji picker open?

Usually the 🌐 key setting changed, another app grabbed the shortcut, or something turned on Secure Keyboard Entry. The full checklist is in Emoji picker not working on Mac?

Why does pressing Fn start Dictation instead?

Press 🌐 key to is set to Start Dictation, often after you picked a Dictation shortcut. Change it back in System SettingsKeyboard, or use Fn + E, which works either way.

Can I set a default skin tone for every emoji?

Not in macOS: it remembers a tone per emoji, after you pick it once. Mojito has a single default skin tone setting.
