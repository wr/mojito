The Mac emoji picker, and the alternatives

Every Mac has one, a shortcut away. Here’s what it does well, where it falls short, and what to try for something faster.

By Wells Riley of Wells Workshop, maker of Mojito·Updated October 5, 2026·Tested on macOS 27

How do I open the emoji picker on a Mac?

Click in a text field and press Fn + E, or Control + Command + Space. The picker opens at your cursor: type to search, then press Return or click.

It’s a system panel, not an app, so there’s nothing in Applications. No shortcut? Choose EditEmoji & Symbols; nearly every app has it.

Mojito lets you skip the panel: type :coffee where you’re writing and press Return.

What the built-in picker can do

  • Search by name or idea: pizza, party, and thumbs all work.
  • Frequently Used: the emoji you pick most float to the top.
  • Skin tones: click and hold an emoji; macOS remembers your choice per emoji.
  • Word to emoji: type heart, press Fn + E, and your pick replaces it.
  • Stickers and Genmoji: custom emoji from a description, on Apple Intelligence Macs.
  • Character Viewer: the expand button (top right) adds thousands of symbols.
  • Favorites: in the expanded viewer, select an emoji and click Add to Favorites.
  • Action menu: add Technical Symbols (⌘ ⌥ ⇧) or clear Frequently Used.

Where it falls short

  • It’s a detour: open, search, pick, and find your place again, for every emoji.
  • No shortcodes: :tada: stays plain text in Mail, Notes, and Messages.
  • Frequently Used runs itself: you can clear it, not arrange it.
  • No default skin tone: you set it one emoji at a time.
  • No GIFs: see how to send GIFs on a Mac.
  • Secure Keyboard Entry blocks it, and every third-party picker too.

Alternatives to the Mac emoji picker

  • Mojito (ours, free and open source): : for emoji, :: for symbols, and ::: for GIFs, at your cursor.
  • Rocket and Comoji (free, closed source): inline : pickers too. A one-time $10 Rocket Pro adds saved GIFs and snippets; Comoji adds /gif.
  • Raycast and Alfred: launchers. Raycast’s free emoji search shows results in its own window; Alfred expands exact :codes: with its paid Powerpack.

Emoji picker questions

Can I replace the Mac emoji picker?

Yes, and keep the shortcut. In Mojito’s SettingsGeneral, click Replace System Picker, and ⌃ ⌘ Space and the 🌐 key open its emoji browser instead.

What changed in macOS 26 and 27?

Mostly the look: macOS 26 restyled it in Apple’s glass material (macOS 27 keeps it), and the shortcuts haven’t changed since macOS 14. macOS 26.4 added Emoji 17.0 (🫍 🪊 🪎), which can show as empty boxes on older devices.

Why won’t the emoji picker open?

Check the Press 🌐 key to setting, look for another app using ⌃ ⌘ Space, and rule out Secure Keyboard Entry. Step-by-step fixes: Emoji picker not working on Mac?
