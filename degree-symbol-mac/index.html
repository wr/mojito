How to type the degree symbol on a Mac (°)

Shift-Option-8, in any app. Here’s that, the two look-alikes to avoid, and the keys for other layouts.

By Wells Riley of Wells Workshop, maker of Mojito·Updated October 5, 2026·Tested on macOS 27

⇧shift ⌥option 8 → °

How do I type the degree symbol on a Mac?

Press Shift + Option + 8. For a temperature, put the letter right after it: 72°F.

It works in every app because it’s part of the keyboard layout, like the em dash. Leave out Shift and you get a bullet (•) instead, the most common near miss.

Mojito lets you type ::deg and press Return instead, on any keyboard layout.

Other ways to type it

  • Character Viewer. Press Fn + E, click the expand button (top right), search degree, and double-click °. Skip the one-character °C and °F it also lists.
  • Text replacement. In System SettingsKeyboardText Replacements…, map ;deg to °. Syncs to your iPhone, but only works in Apple’s apps.
  • Google Docs. The shortcut works there too, or choose InsertSymbolsSpecial characters and enter 00B0.
  • Keyboard Viewer. Turn on Show Input menu in menu bar in System SettingsKeyboardText InputEdit…, then choose Show Keyboard Viewer from it. Hold Shift and Option to see where ° is on any layout.
  • Shortcode. With Mojito, type ::degree in any app, or just ::deg and pick it from the list.

°, º, or ˚? Don’t grab the look-alike

MarkNameMac shortcutUnicodeWhat it’s for
°Degree sign⇧ ⌥ 8U+00B0Temperatures, angles, coordinates
ºMasculine ordinal indicator⌥ 0U+00BASpanish and Portuguese ordinals: 1º, 2º
˚Ring above⌥ KU+02DAA standalone accent, the ring from å
  • They won’t match a search for °, and some cleanup tools turn 72º into 72o.
  • To spot one, press ⌘ F and search for a real °. Anything that looks like one but doesn’t highlight is an impostor.

Should °F be one character or two?

Two: ° then a capital F or C. Unicode advises against the one-character ℉ and ℃, and not every app’s search treats them as equal.

What if my keyboard isn’t US English?

  • US, ABC, British, Australian, Canadian English, Dutch: ⇧ ⌥ 8
  • German: ⇧ ^
  • Swiss German: ⇧ §
  • French: ⇧ )
  • Spanish: ⇧ ⌥ º (the º key alone types the look-alike)
  • Italian: ⇧ à

Not listed? The Keyboard Viewer shows where ° is on yours.

How do I type the degree symbol on iPhone?

On the 123 keyboard, press and hold 0, then slide to °.

More degree questions

How do I write coordinates and angles?

Use ° with the prime (′) and double prime (″), not an apostrophe and quote: 40°41′21″N. Type ::prime and ::double_prime in Mojito, or see Math symbols on a Mac.

What’s the HTML code for the degree symbol?

&deg; or &#176; for ° (U+00B0). In CSS content, use "\00B0".
